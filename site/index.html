A few steps to Homebrew

Take the local route.

Download and extract the offline bundle. Open a terminal in its folder, then run:

python3 serve.py --host 192.168.1.10

Replace the example address with your computer's LAN IPv4. Open the printed URL, enter the TV's IP, and accept pairing.

When the TV reports success: turn Quick Start+ Off, reboot, and confirm Root OK in Homebrew Channel.

Keeping the TV offline during setup

Block the TV's Internet access at your router while allowing LAN traffic. Keep the computer's local server running until setup finishes. Use the printed LAN URL; localhost would point the TV back to itself. An existing TV app may still attempt network requests, so the router rule is what keeps the TV offline.

Pairing or certificate trouble?

On your computer, open https://TV-IP:3001/ and accept the certificate exception for your own TV, then retry. Both devices must be on the same LAN without client isolation. Allow the local server through your computer's private-network firewall; do not forward its port to the Internet.

Selective controls

Targets ACR/viewing collection, LG voice services, advertising, diagnostics and firmware updates. Preserves general networking and picture/audio processing.

One file to install

Copy privacy.py to the rooted TV. Check compatibility, install, reboot and verify. Telnet stays unchanged unless you explicitly disable it.

A way back

Private backups and reversible overlays support restore. Optional consents stay declined, and old diagnostic uploads stay quarantined.

Experimental installer. The individual controls were tested on the original G5. The packaged installer still needs a clean-TV install/reboot/restore test. Keep the physical microphone switch Off. These checks do not prove that all firmware telemetry or every possible microphone path is blocked.

What leaves your network?

This site adds no analytics, external fonts or automatic log uploads. Downloading this site and bundle contacts GitHub's hosting infrastructure from your computer. Once extracted, the bundle runs locally and the TV fetches its rooting files from your computer's LAN server.

When you run the local launcher, your TV's pairing key is stored in that browser's local storage. Pairing and launch commands go directly to the TV over the LAN. Existing firmware and apps have their own network behavior.
