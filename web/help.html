Connect locally

Keep the server running. Open the exact http://LAN-IP:PORT/ address printed by serve.py on your computer. Do not use localhost: the TV would then try to download from itself.

Enter the TV's LAN IPv4 address. Both devices must be on the same LAN, with client isolation disabled. The TV does not need Internet access.

If pairing fails due to a certificate error, open https://TV-IP:3001/ on this computer and accept the certificate exception for your own TV. Then return and retry. Do not disable browser security globally.

Allow this server through your computer's firewall for your private LAN only. Do not forward its port on your router.

After successful rooting, disable Quick Start+, reboot, and confirm Root OK in Homebrew Channel. Root logs stay at /tmp/dangbro-root.log on the TV.
